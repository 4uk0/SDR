Our story

It started
in a garage

I grew up in a family of bikers, painters and mechanics. From a young age, the smell of body filler, resin and primer was a part of my life. My father and uncle still ride, build and paint motorcycles to this day.

At 6, I got my first scooter. At 9, I worked on a 1950s Jawa with my grandfather — sanded it, did the bodywork, and learned to spray my first metallic paint. That was the beginning of a passion for custom paint, motorcycles and building things my own way.

Years later, the smell of primer still gets me going. What started as a kid's project in my grandfather's garage became SDR — Slammed Dirty Rides — a small shop in Florida where every bike gets the same attention I gave that old Jawa.

I don't do production paint. Every job starts with a conversation: what you ride, how you ride it, and what you want people to feel when they see it. Then come the hours — bodywork, masking, mixing, spraying — candy over metallic, lace and honeycomb patterns, freehand airbrush, until it's exactly right. No shortcuts, and never two bikes the same.

Same passion. Different tools. If you've got a ride that needs getting dirty, let's talk.

— Kai
