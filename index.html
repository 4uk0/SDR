01 — THE STORY

BUILT IN
THE GARAGE.

SDR_AUTO grew from a family where motorcycles, paint and mechanics were part of everyday life.

Growing up in a family of bikers, painters and mechanics shaped SDR from the beginning. My father and uncle still ride, build and paint motorcycles today.

I remember waking up on weekends and holidays and walking straight into the garage. The whole house smelled of body filler, fiberglass resin and primer. A huge connected garage linked my father's home with my grandfather's — it became the place where I learned to watch, ask questions and eventually get my hands dirty.

I was six when I got my first motorcycle: a little Yamaha scooter that my father custom painted for me. I rode it everywhere.

At around nine, I got my first big motorcycle — an old 1950s Jawa. It was little more than a frame, tank and seat: no fairings, no fenders and no lights. My grandfather helped me take it apart and taught me sanding and bodywork.

An older friend gave me a jar of solvent-based grey metallic paint. My grandfather sprayed the tank with a pot spray gun and showed me how metallic paint should be laid down. When that finish caught the sunlight, I was hooked.

“That was the beginning of a passion for custom paint, motorcycles and building things my own way.”

The Jawa would run for a day and then spend three or four days broken while my father brought it back to life. Those early rides, repairs and experiments became the foundation of SDR_AUTO.
